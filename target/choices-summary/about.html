Making thoughtful choices online

Every day we make choices about what to share, what to trust, and how to treat other people online. A few careful habits can make digital spaces safer and more useful.

Three useful habits

  • Protect your privacy. Share only information that is appropriate for the audience, and keep passwords private.
  • Check before you trust. Look for reliable sources and confirm surprising claims before passing them on.
  • Be considerate. Communicate respectfully and pause before posting something that could harm or embarrass someone.
